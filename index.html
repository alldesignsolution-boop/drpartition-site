Common questions

Prices, scope and timing

Straight answers before you call.

How much does a gypsum partition wall cost per sq ft?

Our gypsum partition starts from RM6.90 per sq ft of wall area for jobs in Klang Valley and PJ. That covers metal studs, a single layer of gypsum board on both faces, and skimming. The final rate depends on wall height, total area and site conditions, and is confirmed in a written quote after a site measure.

What is included in the partition price?

Supply and installation of the metal stud frame, one layer of gypsum board on each face, joint treatment and skimming, ready for painting. Doors, glass panels, sound insulation, painting and double-layer board are quoted separately.

How much does a plaster ceiling cost per sq ft?

Plaster ceiling starts from RM4.80 per sq ft of ceiling area. Light troughs, cove lighting, L-boxes and access panels are quoted separately.

Is there a minimum job size?

Yes. We take jobs from RM 8,000. That is roughly 1,150 sq ft of partition wall, or a mix of partition, ceiling and electrical work.

Which areas do you cover?

Klang Valley, including Petaling Jaya, Kuala Lumpur and the surrounding areas.

How long does the work take?

It depends on the area and the mix of work. With about 40 workers we can put a full crew on your site, and every written quote comes with a work schedule.

Do you work with interior design firms?

Yes. Interior design firms and main contractors can ask for our trade rate. You get one contact person and quotes within 24 hours of receiving your drawings.
