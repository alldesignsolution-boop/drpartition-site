Before the site visit

Getting your electrical plan right

Changing a light or socket after the ceiling and walls are finished costs time and money. A few decisions up front avoid most of it:

1. Mark your furniture layout

Sockets follow desks, counters and display units. A rough layout is enough for us to count points.

2. List equipment that needs its own supply

Air-cond units, ovens, chillers, servers, printers and signage often need dedicated circuits. Tell us about them early.

3. Decide the lighting style

Downlights, track lights, cove lighting and feature lights each need different cut-outs in the ceiling. Deciding now means the ceiling is built around them.

4. Check your building's rules

Many malls and office towers have their own requirements for fit-out electrical work. Send us the fit-out guide from your building management and we will plan around it.

Electrical questions

Common questions

Straight answers before you call.

How is electrical work for an office or shop priced?

Electrical work is quoted after a site visit, itemised by lighting points, switch and socket points, cable runs and DB box works. The count comes from your layout, so the more complete your plan, the faster and more accurate the quote.

Why do electrical works together with partition and ceiling?

Cables have to run inside the walls and above the ceiling before the boards are closed. When one team does all three, wiring is pulled at the right moment and nothing has to be cut open again.

What should I prepare before the site visit?

A floor plan with your furniture layout, where you want lights, switches and power points, and any equipment that needs its own supply such as air-cond, ovens, servers or signage.

Who does the electrical work?

Our electrical works are carried out by a PW4 wireman, certified for single-phase and three-phase wiring. That covers the lighting, power points and DB box works a typical office or shop fit-out needs.

Is there a minimum order for electrical work?

Our minimum job is RM 8,000. Most clients combine electrical work with partition and ceiling works for their fit-out, which usually brings the job above the minimum.
