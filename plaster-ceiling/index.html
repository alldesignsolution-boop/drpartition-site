Before you get quotes

Planning your ceiling

Most ceiling problems come from deciding things too late. Settle these before the board goes up:

1. Where do the lights go?

Mark downlights, cove lights and feature lights on a plan. Cut-outs made during installation are neater and cheaper than cutting a finished ceiling.

2. Where are the air-cond units and valves?

Anything that needs servicing needs an access panel underneath it. We will ask about this at the site measure.

3. What ceiling height do you want?

Lower ceilings are cheaper to cool; higher ceilings feel more open. We check the slab height and any beams or ducting before quoting.

4. Who does the walls and wiring?

When partition, ceiling and wiring are done by one team, cables are pulled before the ceiling closes and walls are finished to the ceiling line. See our gypsum partition and electrical pages.

Ceiling questions

Common questions

Straight answers before you call.

How much is a plaster ceiling per sq ft in Malaysia?

Our plaster ceiling starts from RM4.80 per sq ft of ceiling area in Klang Valley and PJ, for a flat suspended gypsum ceiling, jointed and skimmed. Most jobs land between RM4.80 and RM5.50 per sq ft depending on ceiling height, layout and site access. Light troughs, cove lighting, L-boxes and access panels are quoted separately.

How do I measure ceiling area?

For a flat ceiling it is usually the same as the floor area of the rooms you want covered. A 30 ft by 60 ft unit is 1,800 sq ft. Drop-downs, troughs and bulkheads are measured separately.

What is the difference between plaster ceiling and gypsum ceiling?

In Malaysia the two names are usually used for the same thing: gypsum board fixed to a suspended metal frame, then jointed and skimmed so it looks like one smooth surface. It is different from a grid ceiling with lift-out tiles.

Can I still reach the air-cond and wiring above the ceiling?

Yes, if access panels are planned. We mark where air-cond units, valves and junction boxes sit and add access panels there. Tell us early so they go in before the ceiling is closed.

Is there a minimum order?

Yes, jobs start from RM 8,000. A ceiling-only job usually needs around 1,700 sq ft or more; smaller ceilings can be combined with partition or electrical work.
